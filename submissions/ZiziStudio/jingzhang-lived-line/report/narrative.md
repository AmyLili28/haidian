# THE LIVED LINE — field evidence protocol

This supporting note is a protocol, not a record of completed fieldwork.

## Two site walks

1. Daytime: publicly accessible areas around Dazhongsi Station toward the everyday-life section between Xueyuan South Road and Zhichun Road.
2. Night: publicly open sections between the Dayuncun football field and Qinghua East Road; if closed or under construction, use a continuously open alternative nearby.

Only publicly open space may be entered. Fences, closed sites, and construction areas are excluded. Night fieldwork prioritizes personal safety and should be completed with a companion where possible.

## Evidence classes

- Direct observation: a concrete action witnessed at a stated time and place.
- Consented interview statement: a short anonymous statement recorded after explaining the purpose and obtaining verbal consent.
- Public information: an official or otherwise registered public source.
- AI/researcher inference: a hypothesis that must never be presented as resident demand.

## Minimum baseline before finalization

- At least 20 specific observation records, including at least 8 at night.
- At least 5 anonymous interviews with consent and read-back confirmation.
- At least 22 original photographs, with zero identifiable faces, children's faces, or licence plates in any published image.
- At least 3 non-AI work orders and 3 candidate AI work orders with baseline, risk, Human Review, metric, and exit condition.

Each observation may carry one or more analytical tags: Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture, Urban AI Life Experience, AI Convergence and Innovation; and health, education, commerce, mobility, public space, maintenance, or accessibility. A tag means only that the observation is relevant to a planning question. It does not prove the full 43.6-square-kilometre system or any Zone/Wing strategy. If a health, education, or commerce interface is not observed, the record states “not observed in this walk” rather than inventing a demand.

The two walks test the eastern Scenario Enablement logic at real public-life interfaces and the Dazhongsi Five-Minute Proof Gate. They do not validate the official locations or boundaries of all Three Zones and Two Wings. The western Technology Services Wing and ecosystem chain remain system-level proposals supported by public sources and must be developed through separate stakeholder and professional research.

## Non-extrapolation

The sample only supports wording such as “during two site walks, we observed…”. It does not represent the approximately 450,000 residents reported to be connected to the park, and it must not be reported as a population percentage or general public preference.

## Lived Evidence Receipt

Every candidate scenario is recorded as:

`observation ID → observed fact → non-AI first repair → minimum AI increment → beneficiary → potential harm → Human Review → baseline and threshold → exit result`

If the non-AI repair completely resolves the problem, the AI increment is removed. If a scenario lacks a human owner, appeal route, offline fallback, or stop condition, it cannot enter the spatial proposal.
