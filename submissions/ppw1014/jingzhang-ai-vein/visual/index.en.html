AI governance evidence & lighting rule

Governing rule "ordinary first, overlay second; no G0, no light": the 3 TVS completed G0 deterministic tabletop replays (24 decision points, 24/24 expected=actual, all four branch types covered, fail-closed 15/15, zero model calls / field activity / personal data), plus the no-account seven-rights contract and non-AI parity coverage.

G0 evidence

G0 replay evidence

  • 24 decision points, expected=actual 24/24 (tvs_g0_replay_evidence.json)
  • Branch coverage stop 11 · hold 4 · recover 3 · proceed 6 (all four types)
  • Fail-closed enforcement 15/15 — every stop/hold is a human-review point
  • Honesty declaration: zero model calls, zero field activity, zero personal data

No-account rights contract

  • Seven rights (non_ai_parity_contract.json): account-free use · no forced QR · non-AI same-task route · low-disturbance operation · human stop authority · appeal & correction · minimisation without profiling
  • 12/12 scenario cards carry the equivalent-human-service statement
  • Coverage recomputed from countable in-package content only

Lighting ladder

  • E0 concept, unlit ×4 (SC-03/06/09/12)
  • E1 pilot-ready ×5 (SC-01/04/05/07/10): G0 passed + human channel in place
  • E2 pilot-validated ×3 (SC-02/08/11 TVS): real operation needs separate permits

TVS Testable Service Blueprints

SC-02, SC-08, and SC-11 show the full chain in prose, boards, and this page: persona / trigger / spatial node, frontstage-backstage, input/output and lawful sources, minimum collection, human review, fail-closed, complaints/appeals, operating responsibility, pilot gate, steps, exit, and verifiable metrics. Older, disabled, and non-smart-device users get equivalent service via a human counter, phone, or paper forms.

SC-02 · SC-08 · SC-11

SC-02 Red-Team Sandbox

  • Trigger: booking/human counter; node: Zhongzhiyuan governance hall
  • Backstage: isolated-environment validation, red-team execution, expert review → restricted report
  • Governance: data stays in sandbox, halt on anomaly, on-site complaints/written appeals
  • Pilot: gate (ownership/authorized sets/safety lead) → register → baseline → test → review → results
  • Metrics: completion rate, critical-issue reproduction, false-positive rate, appeal closure time

SC-08 Data-Factor Parlor

  • Trigger: online booking/counter; node: Dazhongsi parlor
  • Backstage: identity/authorization check, minimal field mapping, sandbox query, compliance review
  • Governance: no raw-data copies, human compliance officer, delist on anomaly, counter complaints
  • Pilot: gate (lawful source/use/retention/owner) → register → minimize → sandbox demo → review → audit
  • Metrics: compliance pass rate, field-minimization ratio, delisting time, appeal closure rate

SC-11 Shuttle Test Corridor

  • Trigger: approved test application/on-site service point; node: restricted corridor
  • Backstage: route validation, pre-shift checks, confined operation, remote monitoring, incident review
  • Governance: safety officer + remote takeover; stop if location/comm/safety officer fail
  • Pilot: gate (legal permit/confined conditions/insurance/contingency) → pre-shift → empty → passenger → review
  • Metrics: takeovers per 1,000 km, minimal-risk stop success, pre-shift pass rate, complaint closure time
