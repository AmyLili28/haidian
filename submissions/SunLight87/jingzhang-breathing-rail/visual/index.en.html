What “Strata” means

“Strata” places railway heritage, public life, innovation and trustworthy intelligence in legible spatial layers. “Re-layering” makes every intervention verifiable, correctable, reversible and upgradable.

The design therefore offers a workspace for comparing candidates against the same evidence, rather than a single automated answer.

ONE DECISION SENTENCE / 一条决策句Read the source, test the constraint; pilot small, then decide whether to diffuse.

03 / STRATA + LOOP

Four urban layers, one runtime feedback loop

The layers are not a technology stack. They are the evidence and responsibility path every spatial intervention must cross. The loop spans all four layers and pauses or rolls back by default when evidence is missing.

four layers / one loop

Heritage Substrate Layer · 遗产基底层

Railway heritage, public memory, existing open space and responsibility boundaries; new proposals sit beside verified evidence.

Public Stitching Layer · 公共缝合层

Connects everyday paths, groups and services into interfaces that are legible, accessible and handable to a person.

Innovation Collaboration Layer · 创新协作层

Organises resource import, capability diffusion, testing, shared learning and professional collaboration with clear inputs and owners.

Trustworthy Intelligence Layer · 可信智能层

Makes data, models and services explainable, contestable, pausable and rollback-ready, with static and staffed alternatives.

Runtime Feedback Loop

From cleared data to public service and back to calibration. Each round leaves source, confidence, human judgement and version records.

05 / AI DESIGN OPERATING SYSTEM

AI generates candidates; people decide what enters the city

Intelligence is not automatic approval. Reusable intelligence comes from fixed inputs, constraint checks, candidate-family comparison, independent critique and rollback records—not from one apparently certain generated image.

machine candidate → human decision
01 / INTAKE

Clear the input

Collect sources, permissions, spatial relations, user pain points and unknowns; do not silently fill gaps.

02 / CONSTRAINT

Check constraints

Check heritage, accessibility, movement, privacy, fire safety, data permission and provisional-geometry limits.

03 / GENERATE

Generate a family

Generate comparable relations, route prompts, components or time-slot combinations—not one answer.

04 / CRITIQUE

Independent critique

Professional and public reviewers question access, inclusion, risk and evidence consistency.

05 / HUMAN GATE

HumanDecision

An accountable person records grounds, status, trade-offs, pause, rejection, rollback or approval; AI does not sign.

06 / LEARN

Pilot and rollback

Run a small reversible pilot with a staffed alternative, stop button, feedback record and sunset trigger.

06 / WHY THIS PLACE

Turn six site breaks into six design moves

The linear continuity, cross-connections and multiple owners around the Jing-Zhang heritage park call for continuity with interfaces. Strata turns technical limits into public-space experience.

problem → response
01

Break in heritage legibility

One Base retains time, source and memory; guides and data plaques show versions, not unauthorised portraits or invented stories.

02

Break between park and neighbourhood

Three Fields organise continuity, cross-connections and daily service; relationship lines are interfaces to verify, not new roads, bridges or stations.

03

Break in data trust

Co-locate and calibrate in the north field, label confidence, record model cards and red-team outliers; uncalibrated readings do not become regulatory or medical claims.

04

Break in automated service

Translate model outputs in the civic field into paper, voice, static and staffed equivalents; if people cannot leave, do not publish.

05

Break between innovation and adoption

Test movable, pausable service prototypes in the adoption field; decide whether to adopt only after feedback, while engineering and property checks remain separate.

06

Break in governance responsibility

Two wings bring standards, legal, operations and scenario capability; every prototype binds an owner, stop condition, rollback path and review record.
