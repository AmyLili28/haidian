# Formal Narrative / 正式叙事报告

## 修订摘要 / Revision summary

本报告与 `proposal.md`、`proposal.en.md` 同步更新，母概念核定为 **《百年京张·城市叠代》 / JINGZHANG STRATA**，副标题为“从铁路遗产到城市智能基础设施 / From Railway Heritage to Civic Intelligence Infrastructure”，口号为“百年为基，城市叠代 / A Centennial Infrastructure Re-layered for Civic Intelligence.” 仓库 slug `jingzhang-breathing-rail` 保持为技术标识，不作为品牌使用。

The readable narrative now uses **JINGZHANG STRATA / 百年京张·城市叠代** as the public concept. Its central proposition is a heritage version base, three relay fields (Zhongzhiyuan Calibration Field, AI Origin Community Civic Translation Field, and Dazhongsi Adoption Field), two service/validation wings, and twelve reversible scenario prototypes S01–S12. The former “Breathing Rail/Public Air Rail” is retained only as a blue-green and environmental-sensing module label.

“叠代 / strata” is defined as layering railway heritage, public life, innovation industry, and trustworthy intelligence, then re-layering through verification, correction, withdrawal, public participation, professional review, and `HumanDecision`. AI generates constrained option families rather than a single answer; all scenes retain human fallback, stop, complaint, rollback, and exit paths. Reinforcement learning, digital twins, and DRL are explicitly future isolated experiments, not current capabilities.
