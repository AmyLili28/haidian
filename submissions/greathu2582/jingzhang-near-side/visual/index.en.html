Precision warning：All geometry uses the repository provisional boundary (official_boundary=false, geometry_role=provisional_constraint) for generation, self-check, visualisation and design discussion only; it is not an official redline, approval basis or precise-area basis. A repository background cross-check found a 533-898 m (mean 667 m) consistent offset between the provisional boundary and OSM centrelines of the announced boundary streets. The inferred spine is a measurement baseline for method demonstration, not the surveyed park alignment. Everything is recalculated once official data is published.

Assumptions and data gaps

  • A-BOUNDARY-001 — All submitted geometry uses the repository provisional boundary; the official site boundary, key-area polygons, road redlines and park edge face are not yet publicly available.
    Area, ratio and distance metrics are design-model outputs subject to recalculation when official data is published; they are not official redlines or precise-area bases.
  • A-SPINE-001 — The inferred green spine is a measurement baseline generated by this proposal for method demonstration; it is not claimed to be the actual Jing-Zhang Heritage Park alignment.
    Near-side units, the three rings and all distance-dependent metrics must be recalculated along the official park edge face when available.
  • A-MASSING-001 — Building footprints, storey assumptions and building footprint coverage are conceptual low-confidence design quantities, not statutory building scale or development controls.
    FAR, building height, building density and setbacks remain unknown pending official regulatory controls.
  • A-OSM-001 — The OSM background cross-check is volunteer-contributed data with uneven coverage and precision.
    Offset readings are used only to explain provisional-boundary uncertainty, not to replace or upgrade the official boundary.
