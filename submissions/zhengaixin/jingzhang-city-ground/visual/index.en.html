G0-G4 thick-ground grammar

The five bands are not five separate land uses. They organize one urban ground from public movement to service support.

G0 MOVEcontinuous, legible and accessible public movement
G1 STAYshade, seating, stormwater and seasonal comfort
G2 THRESHOLDporches, in-between space and openable edges
G3 SHARElearning, work, community and public activity
G4 SERVICEmaintenance, delivery, equipment and reversible AI

Implementation tools and evidence boundary

Intervention escalates from light repair to structural change. A problem solvable through a small move does not go directly to demolition or new construction.

13 components + L1/L2/L3

  • L1: seating, shade, signs, surface repair, bicycle parking and small rain gardens
  • L2: entrances, porches, forecourts, courtyards, retail and open ground-floor interfaces
  • L3: adaptive reuse, station-city restructuring and conditional selective new insertion

Verify before formal development

  • official GIS, road and rail technical data
  • building condition, ownership, fire and utilities
  • passenger flow, parking, cost, operator and funding
  • privacy, safety, accessibility and public feedback
