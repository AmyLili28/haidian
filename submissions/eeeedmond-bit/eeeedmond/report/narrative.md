# 京张新栈·轨旁未来｜执行摘要

京张新栈是一套沿百年京张遗产公共廊道展开、可进入园区和建筑内部的城市站台系统。平台基座提供结构、能源、网络、无障碍和公共停留条件；固定 AI 核心提供模型、边缘算力、设备端口与人工接管；可替换插件承载协同开发、轻型验证、产品体验、公共服务、文化活动和日常经营。

总体结构保持“主脊贯通—核心带动—节点植入—横链织网”。众智园强化全栈研发和轻型验证，AI 原点社区强化协作转化与企业成长，大钟寺强化发布、商业共创和市场反馈；中关村科技服务翼连接资本、知识产权和专业服务，小月河场景赋能翼连接城市应用和反馈。

实施从三个独立试点包开始，依次通过需求、场地权利、专业安全核查和运营责任四道门槛。所有开放的 AI 插件必须有实名责任人、版本记录、清晰告知、人工接管、停用和空间恢复方式；基本公共服务保留人工或非 AI 通道。真实运营形成基线后，再设需求响应、重复使用、产品改进、商户参与和年度人次目标。

组织方未提供的法定边界、建筑普查、控规、权属、市政和交通基线不被伪装成实测结论。项目工作边界、概念图层和容量预估均标明来源、公式、置信度及正式资料到位后的复算路径。

## Executive Summary

JZ RE:STACK is a family of urban platforms deployed along the Centennial Jing-Zhang heritage corridor and connected to campuses and existing buildings. A platform base provides structure, energy, network, accessibility and public space; a fixed AI core provides models, edge computing, device interfaces and human takeover; replaceable plug-ins support co-development, lightweight validation, product experience, public service, culture and everyday commerce.

The spatial rule is consistent: spine continuity, core activation, node insertion and cross-link networking. Zhongzhiyuan focuses on full-stack R&D and lightweight validation; the AI Origin Community on collaboration, translation and enterprise growth; Dazhongsi on launch, commercial co-creation and market feedback. The Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing supplies capital, IP and professional support, while the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing returns urban applications and feedback.

Implementation begins with one independently reviewable pilot package in each key area. Demand, site rights, professional safety checks and accountable operation are mandatory entry gates. Every open AI plug-in requires named ownership, versioning, notice, human takeover, shutdown and spatial restoration. Basic services retain human or non-AI access. Performance targets are calibrated only after a real operating baseline exists.
