Assumptionsassumptions.json

A-CONTROLS-001 pending_professional_confirmation

Detailed regulatory controls (FAR, building height, density, setback, road redline), ownership, municipal utilities, and engineering constraints are missing from public sources and must be confirmed from official attachments before statutory use.

A-BOUNDARY-001 provisional_constraint

SITE-001 and KEY-A1/A2/A3 use the site package's provisional rough polygons (official_boundary=false, boundary_precision=provisional_rough). All areas, ratios and counts derived from them are provisional design-model values with low confidence.

A-RAIL-001 design_assumption

The sandbox contains no surveyed rail alignment. The 120 m Chain Band axis (CONSTRAINTS-RAIL) is a design assumption used only to organize the zipper concept; the actual Jingzhang heritage rail alignment and heritage control lines are not represented.

A-BLDG-001 method_only

No existing-building survey, ownership or regulatory parcel data is available. geometry/buildings.geojson contains proposed massing envelopes only (design_action='新增（建议体量包络）').

A-IMAGE-001 conceptual_illustration

The 13 teeth scene images under assets/media/teeth/ are AI-generated conceptual illustrations (Lovart AI, free model nano-banana-pro) with verbatim prompts archived in report/copyright_statement.md Appendix A (SHA-256 per prompt) and machine-readable trainedAlgorithmicMedia XMP preserved. They express design intent only.

A-METRICS-001 low_confidence_design_model

The three formal core visual metrics (site_area_sqm, green_ratio, public_space_ratio) are recomputed from submitted provisional geometry in EPSG:4548; they are finite and reproducible but retain low confidence and a provisional role.

A-PHASING-001 conceptual_suggestion

The three-phase plan and renewal project list (JZ-01..JZ-06) are conceptual suggestions without funding, implementation bodies or approval paths.
