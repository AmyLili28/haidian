Three-Level Scope

Coordinated Research Area: ~43.6 km2 (official text) macro industry and regional-coordination research; Overall Design Area: ~11.4 km2 regulatory-level urban design; Key Areas: ~368.4 ha total (three key zones about 192/104/72 ha). This package focuses on the Qinghe Station TOD Gateway as the northern fulcrum topic.

Land use

Concept land structure (provisional; recompute under the unified caliber after official data): transport-hub land as the core with innovation R&D mixed-use, commercial/service, cultural display and residential support at the periphery; green and public space integrated into the blue-green skeleton. Only recomputable parcel counts and directional indications are charted; no precise percentages are committed.

Transport & slow traffic

Concept of integrated HSR/intercity/metro interchange (layered vertical separation) with elevated corridors to the over-track complex; slow-traffic greenway linking the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park and Shangdi / Zhongguancun Software Park; Xiaoyue River corridor to be surveyed. High-risk scenarios (seamless security / crowd guidance) set statutory-actor assumption, minimal data, retention-deletion, appeal, human takeover and pilot exit conditions.

Blue-green & public space

Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park as the green spine with the "rail memory trail - station square - software park greenway" continuous open sequence, four-level transition and the juxtaposition "weight of a century of rail / lightness of the AI age"; reversible public-space component library under joint control.

Buildings

Retain-renovate-demolish all in parallel: rail and industrial-heritage concept elements retained and filed, low-efficiency offices/warehouses converted to innovation service space, demolition limited to valueless temporary or informal stock; over-track value feeds back into public-space operation.

Renewal projects

Three-phase renewal project list with RACI: near term (1-3y) square & underground integrated renovation, mobility and event-booking scenario pilots, HSR pop-up expo; mid term (3-5y) rail-over innovation complex, greenway through-connection, Shuhe community stitch, with the rail-over convergence forum and station-front weekend market forming the annual program system; long term (5-10y) gateway fully formed and fully linked with the Three Districts & Two Wings. Stop conditions, exit mechanisms and withdrawal paths are set.

AI scenarios

12 scenario cards, 3 industry test scenarios, 8 scenario nodes and 3 AI landmarks across transport, industry, space, public service, culture and governance; five personas. AI governance three-phrase rule: anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance; with model-evaluation / data-quality / error-stratification / runtime-monitoring technical protocols.
