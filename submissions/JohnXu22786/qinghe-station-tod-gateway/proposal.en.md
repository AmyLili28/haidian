**Overall Concept (concept-level)**

Primary name "Qinghe Pivot Realm" (QPR). "Pivot" means hub, pivot and critical support; "Realm" is the civic realm that carries a sense of place: anchored on the real Qinghe HSR station along the belt, the concept turns HSR "arrival" into innovation "convergence" and forms the northern gateway of the "Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt," echoing Haidian "Three Districts, Two Wings." This is an original concept proposal containing no confirmed decisions: the overall structure "one station, one pivot, three-node network" arranges three conceptual nodes - Shuguang Living Room (station-front urban living room), Rail-over Pivot Platform (rail-over innovation service complex), Shuhe Nexus (north-station tech community stitch node) - with "over-track value feeding back into public space" as the public-benefit mechanism balanced by original mechanisms such as the station-city co-creation council window, flash-pilot filing system, and feedback credits. The proposal serves all ages through a "15-minute innovation circle" concept service, maps 12 AI+ scenario cards, 3 industry test scenarios, 8 scenario nodes and 3 AI landmarks across transport, industry, space, public service, culture and governance, all with clear accountability boundaries and human review. All geometry in this package is a provisional concept model to be fully recomputed when official boundaries and survey data are released.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three-level scope framework is organized as "macro research - middle design - micro implementation": the Coordinated Research Area (official text 43.6 km2) focuses on macro research of hub radiation, industry patterns, ecological corridors and regional coordination; the Overall Design Area (official text 11.4 km2) focuses on urban renewal and regulatory-plan-level urban design implementing the Three Districts & Two Wings structure; the Key Areas (official text 368.4 ha total) comprise three key zones - Zhongzhiyuan AI Self-Innovation Acceleration Area, Beijing AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster - plus the Zhongguancun Technology-Service Wing and Xiaoyue River Scenario-Empowerment Wing. This proposal focuses on the "Qinghe Station TOD Gateway" topic as the northern hub gateway of the innovation belt with a conceptual regional coordination loop: connecting northward to Shangdi / Zhongguancun Software Park and the Qinghe cluster in linkage with Beiwei Community renewal; along the Jing-Zhang HSR corridor creating conceptual innovation-factor flow interfaces with Future Science City and Huairou Science City; and southward echoing the Economic-Technological Development Zone and Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei city cluster hub division, forming the conceptual skeleton of "north gateway - central axis - south three districts, multi-wing coordination." The three levels correspond to three deliverables: macro industry report, overall urban design, and node implementation handbook.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Within the Coordinated Research Area (official text 43.6 km2) concept framework, the proposal raises the "hub-driven, chain-through" industry organization idea: using the Qinghe Station gateway to receive Jing-Zhang HSR corridor innovation factors and forming a north-south AI innovation corridor with Shangdi / Zhongguancun Software Park, Zhichun Road and Zhongguancun Avenue, while conceptually connecting Future Science City, Huairou Science City and the Economic-Technological Development Zone so the gateway topic is not isolated from the whole belt. Future-city research focuses on five propositions: carrier supply for the AI full-stack self-innovation system; factor flows of a world-class AI innovation ecosystem (land - capital - talent - compute - data - scenario six factors); spatial experimentation of the AI+ scenario empowerment paradigm; operation models of an intelligent AI-vital city; and public arenas for global AI-governance discourse. The gateway plays the "interface" role: it is both the aggregation interface of intercity innovation factors and the display-experience interface of AI scenarios, feeding back innovation through hub flows. Industry scale, jobs-housing balance and passenger-flow forecasts are all conceptual judgments to be rechecked with published statistics and special studies; they constitute no forecast commitment.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

For the Overall Design Area (official text 11.4 km2), the proposal recommends a regulatory-plan-depth urban design concept framework of "renewal first, stock excavation, rigid-flexible combination": forming a cultural spine along the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park and allocating an innovation space-type spectrum around the Three Districts & Two Wings. For the gateway topic, three concept control threads are proposed at regulatory depth: functional mixing (ground-floor publicness, vertical mixing), over-track development (hub deck and surrounding plots linked), and public-space joint control (square, corridor and greenway under unified control); urban-design-guideline level recommendations cover plot scale, interface alignment, ground-floor function and underground connectivity rules. In the digital-support phase, concept-level AI technical protocols are introduced: model evaluation (phased acceptance via benchmark testing), data quality (caliber and de-identification quality inspection), error stratification (statistics by scenario and demographic, published) and runtime monitoring (real-time tiered alerting), all results for human review. Because official boundaries and regulatory data are not yet published, any statement here on floor-area ratio, building height or road redline is a concept suggestion and not a statutory planning conclusion; control rules and plot indicators await professional deepening after the organizer publishes base data.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The Key Areas (official text 368.4 ha total) detailed design follows "Three Districts & Two Wings": Zhongzhiyuan AI Self-Innovation Acceleration Area stresses R&D acceleration and pilot scenarios, Beijing AI Origin Community stresses first-station experience and community symbiosis, Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster stresses industry services and scenario trading, and the Zhongguancun Technology-Service Wing together with the Xiaoyue River Scenario-Empowerment Wing provide soft support. This proposal focuses on the "Qinghe Station TOD Gateway" northern fulcrum with three conceptual nodes: Node 1 Shuguang Living Room - station-front urban living room organizing arrival ritual and public exchange, hosting AI Landmark 1 "Shuguang Order Hall" (intelligent arrival media interface); Node 2 Rail-over Pivot Platform - rail-over innovation service complex hosting hub commerce, office, testing and innovation services, hosting AI Landmark 2 "Rail-over Light Axis" (link-corridor light installation); Node 3 Shuhe Nexus - north-station tech community stitch node combining community co-creation, shared laboratory and night services, hosting AI Landmark 3 "Realm Ring" (deck-loop viewing installation). The three nodes form the concept sequence "living room - platform - community" around the HSR stationhouse; locations and scales await professional deepening after the published boundary.

Brand and visual-identity direction (concept): the "Pivot Axis Mark" is recommended as the core logo language implying rail, hub and orbit; primary palette of "Jing-Zhang rust + AI electro-blue" with tiered type and icon standards; the same symbol system extends across activities (pop-up expo, forum, market). No trademark search has been completed at concept stage; all names and symbols are treated as internal working codenames. A reversible public-space component library ("Pivot Component Library") covers six reversible components - mobile green walls, reversible stages, modular seating, PV canopies, responsive lighting and modular stages - for rapid assembly and removal; the honor-display system ("Shuguang Honor Wall") records developer contributions and annual figures on replaceable panels published yearly.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Retain-renovate-demolish follows all-three-parallel: rail and industrial-heritage concept elements are registered and filed, keep-all-that-can-be-kept, and demolition applies only to temporary or informal stock with no retention value; all areas are concept calibers to be recomputed after official data. The gateway topic keeps heritage rail and industrial elements around the station (old alignment, signal installations, rail components as concept elements), converts low-efficiency office and warehouse buildings into innovation service space, and demolishes only valueless temporary / informal stock. The land structure centers on transport-hub land with innovation R&D mixed-use, cultural display and residential support land at the periphery; high-intensity front-station mixed development is balanced with public space through the "over-track value feeding back into public space" concept mechanism. All land shares in this chapter follow a single concept caliber (gateway concept scope) as directional proportions rather than precise splits - covering transport hub, business/financial, education/research, retail, residential support, cultural, park/green, R&D and reserve types - with no precise percentage commitment; area, green ratio and public-space ratio carry confidence levels and recompute triggers, to be re-aggregated by professionals under the unified caliber after official boundaries and survey data.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Anchored on the Qinghe Station comprehensive transport hub, the proposal suggests a concept mechanism of integrated interchange among HSR, intercity and metro lines: referencing public interchange information such as the Changping Line Qinghe Station and reserving conceptual interfaces with planned rail lines; front-station three-dimensional connection - the lower level organizes rail interchange and pickup yards, the ground level organizes bus and slow traffic, and elevated corridors connect directly to the over-track complex, forming the "layered, separated, convenient interchange" passenger-flow concept. Municipal works combine utility tunnels and an energy micro-grid concept using hub waste heat to serve surrounding buildings and sponge-city drainage. Public services include a hub service hall, talent service points, a community canteen and shared laboratories with barrier-free and age-friendly corridors for all ages. Seamless security and crowd-flow guidance are high-risk scenarios and must define, before piloting: statutory-actor assumption, minimal data, retention-deletion, misjudgment appeal, human takeover and pilot exit conditions:

| High-risk scenario | Statutory-actor assumption | Minimal data & retention-deletion | Misjudgment appeal | Human takeover | Pilot exit conditions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seamless security | Rail and police act by duty; this proposal does not act for them | Throughput and time aggregates only, no identity/biometric, deleted after real-time window | Appeal & recheck channel with staffed handling | Alarm or anomaly to full human takeover | Withdraw when false-alarm exceeds baseline or authorization boundary changes |
| Crowd-flow guidance | Hub operator authorized by law | Anonymous crowd aggregates, short retention, deleted on expiry | Human review corrects guidance suggestions | Peak & anomaly human dispatch takeover | Stop alerting and switch to manual guidance when limits exceeded |

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park is recommended as the green spine, opening a conceptual slow-traffic link between the Qinghe gateway, the heritage park and Shangdi / Zhongguancun Software Park, forming the continuous open-space sequence "rail memory trail - station square - software park greenway"; blue-green space integrates water corridors such as the Xiaoyue River into sponge rain gardens and shaded places, current water conditions to be confirmed by survey (survey hypothesis). Urban character juxtaposes "the weight of a century of rail with the lightness of the AI age": rail ties and spikes are retained as memory elements, new buildings use light, transparent, changeable interfaces, and night scenes rely on layered information-based smart lighting, rejecting glare wall screens. The front-station public-space sequence is suggested as four-level transition - "arrival square - Shuguang Living Room - platform link - campus wedge green" - shaping the northern gateway landmark image of "on the rail, gate to innovation," paired with a cultural wayfinding (bilingual signage) system that shares the same pivot-symbol family for directional clarity and consistent international communication. Blue-green and public space are brought into joint control by the reversible component library so components can be laid out and removed quickly, letting festival and everyday-quiet states alternate.
