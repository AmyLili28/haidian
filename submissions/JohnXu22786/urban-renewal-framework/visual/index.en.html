Key areas

Three concept nodes map to the three districts: Zone North (Zhongzhiyuan) — UNIT·BLOCK demonstration; Zone Mid (Beijing AI Origin Community) — KEEP·BLOCK coordination demonstration; Zone South (Dazhongsi) — SUPERVISION PAVILION station. All node and district positions are subject to professional review.

Land-use structure

The concept land-use structure uses a single caliber (share of concept parcels over the concept overall design area, same caliber as green_ratio); no other public-green share caliber is shown. Recalculation and redraw follow official data release.

Transport and slow traffic

Slow-traffic first: a continuous slow-traffic axis along the green belt links the three nodes; street sections respond by time of day (morning market / event / daily); green mobility first; no road redline or engineering values are preset.

Blue-green network and public space

A green-belt-as-axis, node-parks-as-pearls system; the blue-green share is displayed in the single green_ratio concept caliber; the reversible RENEW KIT component library supports flexible public space.

Buildings

Three retain-renovate-demolish ledgers (heritage retention / functional replacement / renewal-rebuild) managed as lists; stock buildings are preferentially retained with functional replacement; demolition is limited to case-by-case documented openings; concept building parcels are in geometry/buildings.geojson.

AI+ scenarios

Ten numbered scenario cards (SC-01 to SC-10) form the single unified list (merging the two earlier five-card lists) covering governance, industry, space, public services, culture and transport. All data are anonymized-aggregation-only with human review of key decisions and no over-surveillance; three test protocols (TP-01 to TP-03) are defined in the proposal.
