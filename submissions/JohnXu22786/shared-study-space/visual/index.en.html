Key areas

Node envelopes are conceptual parts, not confirmed building footprints; operational RACI, night-safety and fire preconditions, accessible continuous routes, complaint/appeal flow and stop thresholds are described in the proposal body.

Land-use zoning

Single caliber (national land/sea use guide names): R&D 0802 ~25%, Edu & R&D design 0804 ~9%, Residential 0701 ~13%, Commercial 0901 ~14%, Business/Finance 0902 ~13%, Park/Green 1401 ~26%; the green ratio uses a separate green_space layer and is not interchangeable.

Slow mobility

The slow learning route links the three nodes with rail stations, universities and community walking systems (concept schematic, not a road-engineering plan).

Blue-green public space

Built on public material for the heritage-park green space and the Xiaoyue River waterfront, forming shaded study corners, waterfront reading strips and a slow rest loop (concept); blue/green/heritage lines follow official releases and never cross them; public components (wayfinding, seating, lighting, help points, notice boards, shelf) are combinable and replaceable.

Buildings

All buildings are reuse-first, reversible-light-refurbishment conceptual envelopes (study-pod groups, seminar-pod groups, shared desk zones, class zones), with no floor-area, floor-count, height or seat-count conclusion; FAR and height stay unknown.

Renewal projects

  • Comprehensive centre: STU-HALL (concept lead: public operator and design/engineering team)
  • Study community: STU-VALLEY (concept lead: youth organisation and community co-build, supported by a university volunteer alliance)
  • Community night-study: STU-NIGHT Room (concept lead: sub-district community and volunteer organisations)

Near/medium/long phasing (1-3 / 3-5 / 5-10 years) is a conceptual rhythm, not a development sequence; pilot KPIs and stop thresholds are concept-level.
