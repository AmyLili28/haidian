# STITCH Living Room / STITCH·JZ

## 1. Concept and scope

The proposal uses the Jingzhang heritage green belt as a provisional east-west interface: a place to cross, pause and converse. “One Belt, Three Pieces” names three concept components—Stitch Bridge Court, East-West Market Gallery and Stitch Axis. This is a design concept, not an official plan, redline, approval or construction commitment.

## 2. Three scope levels

The coordination study, overall design and key-area detail are represented by provisional repository geometry. All areas and locations must be recomputed against official GIS/CAD, survey, ownership and statutory planning data before implementation.

## 3. Urban and industry research

The east and west sides are treated as complementary public-life systems. The proposal tests how crossings, markets, small events and shared services can make the green belt a connective interface. It does not assert a future industry, investment or land-use commitment.

## 4. Overall design

The green belt becomes a slow-mobility spine. Existing landscape memory is retained where possible; under-bridge spaces are treated as adaptable public rooms. Land-use polygons, areas and ratios in this package are conceptual machine-checkable outputs, not statutory controls.

## 5. Key-area design

Stitch Bridge Court puts crossing above and gathering below; East-West Market Gallery lets daily programs exchange across the two sides; Stitch Axis carries continuous slow movement and public AI services. The point locations are proposals requiring heritage, planning, accessibility and safety review.

## 6. AI+ public-space scenarios

Five bounded scenarios are proposed: anonymous aggregate crossing-demand sensing; AI-assisted accessible routing with human fallback; facility-condition inspection; anonymous environmental sensing and comfort suggestions; and content/safety alerts routed to human review. No individual identification, persistent tracking or automated consequential decision is proposed.

## 7. Land, buildings and change

The concept follows retain–adapt–remove as a decision sequence. It does not state floor-area ratio, height, demolition quantity, ownership or engineering feasibility. Any intervention must follow survey, heritage review, statutory planning and implementation approvals.

## 8. Mobility and services

Walking and cycling have priority in the concept network. Transit, shuttle and event dispersal are operational hypotheses to be tested. Accessibility, emergency access, utilities and temporary event loads require separate professional checks.

## 9. Blue-green and public space

The green belt, pocket spaces and under-bridge rooms are connected as a legible public-space system. Rain gardens, permeable surfaces and modular temporary facilities are suggestions, not performance guarantees.

## 10. Phasing and annual program

Near term: test crossings and one node. Middle term: connect the axis and market gallery. Long term: refine the network and operating model. Suggested annual programs are the East-West Community Stitch Festival, Under-Bridge Market Month and Slow Tour Day; none is an institutional commitment.

## 11. Metrics and evidence

Metrics are geometry-recomputed concept indicators. The package records provisional boundary area, green area, public-space area, counts and ratios with source files and formulas in `metrics.json`. “Counts are not area shares” applies to the land-use figure. Official data must supersede these values.

## 12. Domestic benchmarks

Beijing Shougang, Shanghai West Bund/Xuhui, Shenzhen Dasha River and Chengdu Mengzhuiwan are text-only comparison prompts. Each has a publisher, original URL/title, access date, claim boundary and reuse terms in `sources.json`. No benchmark image, logo, metric, approval or outcome is imported.

## 13. Rights, risk and references

“Stitch Living Room”, “STITCH·JZ”, “One Belt, Three Pieces” and the logo treatment are provisional concept assets. Their dated, scoped prior-rights review, provenance, permitted use and replacement trigger are recorded in `report/copyright_statement.md`; this is not legal clearance. All public-facing use requires formal clearance.
