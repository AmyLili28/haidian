# STITCH LIVING ROOM - JINGZHANG HERITAGE PARK AI COMMONS AND EAST-WEST STITCHING (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以「缝合客厅（STITCH·JZ）」把京张铁路遗址公园绿带组织为东西缝合的公共界面概念：以遗址公园为缝合界面，组织东西两侧街区的功能互补与公共空间联通，把铁路原线曾长期割裂的两侧转译为可跨越、可停留、可交谈的城市客厅，「一带三件」——缝合桥庭（跨线连桥与桥下广场）、东西市集廊（两侧功能互换的市集走廊）、缝合轴（贯穿东西的慢行缝合轴线与AI公共空间），五类AI+场景（跨线通行需求匿名感知与信号调配、AI无障碍通行辅助、公共空间设施状态巡检AI、环境数据匿名感知与舒适度建议、内容与安全告警人工复核）仅处理匿名聚合数据、关键决策人工复核、禁止过度监控，交通慢行优先、缝合轴贯通东西慢行并加密跨线节点，分期实施+年度活动体系（东西社区缝合节、桥下市集月、慢行探访日）。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Stitch Living Room (STITCH·JZ): using the Jingzhang heritage green belt as a stitching interface, organising functional complementarity and public-space connectivity between the east and west streets, translating the two halves long divided by the railway into a crossable, stayable, conversable urban living room.
- Node 1 - Stitch Bridge Court: crossing bridge and under-bridge plaza enabling continuous east-west slow traffic and activating under-bridge grey space.
- Node 2 - East-West Market Gallery: market corridor with interchangeable functions on both sides, organising neighbourhood markets and community activities as bi-directionally accessible public space.
- Node 3 - Stitch Axis: east-west slow-traffic stitching axis with AI-enhanced public space.
- 5 AI+ scenarios (anonymized crossing-demand sensing and signal allocation, AI-assisted accessible crossing, inspection and maintenance AI for public facilities, anonymized environmental sensing and comfort suggestions, human-reviewed content and safety alerts); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance.
- Slow-traffic-first: the stitch axis links east-west slow traffic with denser crossing nodes; event traffic organised around rail transit plus shuttle services with phased dispersal; annual event brands: east-west community stitch festival, under-bridge market month, slow tour day.
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
