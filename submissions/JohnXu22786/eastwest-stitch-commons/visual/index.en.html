Key areas

Three original nodes form a point-line-plane stitching system, doubling as AI pilgrimage landmarks with an honour system and a reversible public-space component library.

Land use

Concept classification from land_use.geojson: 27 zone polygons, 6 land-use codes; ratios and counts on separate axes, no quantitative meaning, recomputed after official data.

Transport and slow traffic

Slow-traffic-first: the Stitch Axis links east-west slow traffic with denser crossing nodes; existing vs proposed colour-coded; event traffic by rail plus shuttles with phased dispersal.

Blue-green network and public space

Green belt as spine, node parks as pearls; rain gardens and permeable paving; light-touch activation of under-bridge grey space; convertible, modular, recyclable public space.

Buildings

Twelve concept building envelopes (retention-first); building scale stays concept-level only; no FAR or height conclusions.

Renewal projects

Four project classes (passage, space, scenario, operation) phased near 1-3 / medium 3-5 / long 5-10 years, each with lead/collaborate roles, preconditions, review gates, pilot duration, exit conditions, handover and KPIs (concept).

AI scenarios

Twelve AI+ scenario cards (crossing sensing, accessible accompaniment, facility inspection, environmental comfort, human-reviewed safety alarms, etc.) and three industry test scenarios; anonymised aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance; four technical protocols: model evaluation, data quality, error stratification, runtime monitoring.
