AI scenarios and governance baseline

Five AI-augmented services (commute safety hints, venue booking, play supervision, family event matchmaking, feedback summarization) x ten scenario cards; runtime quality control (concept protocol): data quality, model evaluation, error stratification, runtime monitoring. Overriding three clauses: anonymized aggregation only; human review of key decisions; no over-surveillance (no individual-identification tracking; no collection of identifiable minors' information).
