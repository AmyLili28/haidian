# AI SCENARIO CARD & INDUSTRY VALIDATION BELT - SCENARIO ENABLEMENT AND TESTING COMMONS (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以「京张场景场（SCENARIO·JZ · AI场景卡与产业测试验证带）」沿京张铁路原线绿带与遗址公园组织AI+场景赋能新范式的公共空间概念：把不少于10张AI场景卡的编制、展示与迭代，与不少于3个AI产业测试验证场景的公共化实验空间，组织为一条可观摩、可参与、可验证的场景带，「一带三场」——具身智能验证场（户外受控验证环境）、大模型评测场（公开可解释的匿名化能力评测展示空间）、场景卡共创工坊（公众与产业共创场景卡），五类AI+场景（场景卡AI辅助生成与版本管理、测试数据匿名采集与合规脱敏、评测过程可解释记录AI、测试预约与秩序调度AI、内容与安全告警人工复核）仅处理匿名聚合数据、关键决策人工复核、禁止过度监控、不把未成熟技术写成已可全面部署，交通慢行优先、绿带连续慢行轴贯通节点且测试人流与观摩流线分时组织，分期实施+年度活动体系（场景卡开放征集季、AI产业测试开放周、年度场景评测日）。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Jingzhang Scenario Field (SCENARIO.JZ): using the Jingzhang heritage green belt as the backbone to organise AI scenario cards and industry test-and-validation scenarios into an observable, participatory, verifiable public scenario belt.
- Node 1 - Embodied Validation Field: outdoor controlled validation environment for embodied-intelligence testing within fenced safety zones, open to public observation in scheduled windows.
- Node 2 - Model Evaluation Court: public, explainable, anonymized capability-evaluation display space with traceable review records.
- Node 3 - Scenario Card Workshop: public and industry co-creation space for authoring and testing AI scenario cards.
- 5 AI+ scenarios (AI-assisted scenario-card generation and versioning, anonymized test-data collection and compliant de-identification, explainable evaluation recording AI, test reservation and crowd-flow scheduling AI, human-reviewed content and safety alerts); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance, no claim that immature technology is fully deployable.
- Slow-traffic-first: a continuous slow-traffic axis along the green belt links the three nodes; test flows and visitor flows are time-separated; annual events: scenario card open call season, AI testing open week, annual scenario evaluation day.
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
