Overview

Three-Level Scope

Official scope hierarchy (announcement wording): coordinated research area approx. 43.6 km²; overall design area approx. 11.4 km² (this package's geometry is a concept sub-scope at this level; provisional site_boundary polygon approx. 11.41 km²); key areas approx. 368.4 ha (three concept envelopes, not official redlines). Regional partners: Beiwai Community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, Economic-Technological Development Area, Beijing–Tianjin–Hebei direction (concept protocols).

Key Areas

Three nodes form the loop "workshop turns materials — market extends life — station guards daily life": Regeneration Workshop (CDW recovery, sorting, recycled-material display; gallery, sample wall, prototyping); Circular Market (exchange, repair, second life; reversible, time-shared operation); Zero-Waste Station (sorting, on-site food-waste treatment, education; human counter, offline-ready). With three AI landmark design catalogues, an honor display system and an eight-family public-space component library (concept).

Land Use

Concept land-use structure (single caliber, provisional, re-cut after official regulatory plan release; does NOT represent current or statutory land use): Research approx. 30.4%, Park green approx. 27.1%, Business/Finance approx. 15.6%, Commercial approx. 13.4%, Residential approx. 11.1%, Cultural approx. 2.3%.

Mobility

Collection routes integrate with the slow-traffic system: community drop-offs along slow paths form a walking "drop—store—haul" route sharing sections and furniture with shade, seating and wayfinding; closed logistics nodes operate with low-disturbance, off-peak loading; the three nodes prefer public-space adjacency to existing rail stations (concept; no road redline or quantity conclusions).

Blue-Green Network & Public Space

Food-waste—soil—greening loop: compost products return to community gardens and corner green spaces under compliance review; recycled permeable aggregates appear as trial paths (subject to testing and compliance); full offline paper/phone/human paths when devices fail; the component library shares one module, is durable and repairable, and relocates wholesale.

Buildings

Stock-first, light retrofit, reversible, time-shared: nodes reuse existing buildings and public-space edges with small, reversible, maintainable components; FAR, heights and footprints remain unknown (dependent on unpublished official regulatory plans and building surveys); no plot-level demolition areas are proposed.

Renewal Projects

Three concept project families: treatment facilities (workshop/station/on-site food-waste points), collection network (drop-offs/two-level routes/closed logistics/reversible installations), governance mechanisms (EPR cooperation, points & deposits, recycled-material preferential procurement, zero-waste convention & annual disclosure). Phasing: pilot + mechanism sandbox, network closure, Beijing–Tianjin–Hebei synergy; costs as qualitative tiers only (low/medium/high).

AI Scenarios

12 AI+ scenario cards (sorting recognition, dispatch, material tracing, compost monitoring, carbon-effect tracking, bin-full prediction, repair matching, supply–demand matching, disclosure proofreading, visitor flow guidance, compost grading, duty assistance): anonymized aggregates only, human review of key decisions, no individual-identifying tracking; 3 industry test scenarios carry protocols, failure thresholds and complaint/decommissioning mechanisms; six personas and key-population service journeys (digital and human paths of equal rights).
