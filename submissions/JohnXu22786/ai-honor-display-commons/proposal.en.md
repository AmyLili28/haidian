# JINGZHANG GLORY BELT - PUBLIC HONOR DISPLAY SYSTEM AND COMMONS COMPONENT LIBRARY (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以「京张荣耀带（GLORY·JZ）」沿京张铁路原线绿带与遗址公园组织公共荣誉展示体系与可复用公共空间组件库概念：把城市荣誉、创新成果与公共贡献转译为可感知、可参与、可更新的绿带公共空间体验，「一带三园」——荣耀长廊（模块化荣誉展陈带）、里程碑广场（铁路里程碑与创新里程碑装置广场）、贡献者花园（开源与公共贡献署名景观），五类AI+场景（荣誉内容AI策展与去重、多语无障碍讲解、展陈设施巡检维护AI、参与度匿名感知与动线建议、内容审核人工复核）仅处理匿名聚合数据、关键决策人工复核、禁止过度监控，交通慢行优先、绿带连续慢行轴贯通节点，分期实施+年度活动体系（荣誉发布日、开源贡献者感谢周、社区共创节）。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Jingzhang Glory Belt (GLORY·JZ): organising a public honor display system and a reusable commons component library along the heritage green belt, translating urban honors, innovation achievements and public contributions into perceivable, participatory, renewable green-belt public-space experiences.
- Node 1 - Glory Gallery: modular display units along the green belt hosting achievement releases, image/text displays and interactive installations.
- Node 2 - Milestone Plaza: installation plaza themed on railway mileposts and innovation milestones.
- Node 3 - Contributor Garden: signature landscape for open-source and public contributions, with renewable name plaques.
- 5 AI+ scenarios (honor-content AI curation and de-duplication, multilingual accessible guided interpretation, inspection and maintenance AI for display facilities, anonymized participation sensing and route suggestions, human-reviewed content moderation); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance.
- Slow-traffic-first continuous green-belt axis; event traffic organised around rail transit plus shuttle services with phased dispersal; annual event brands: honor launch day, open-source contributor thanks week, community co-creation festival.
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
