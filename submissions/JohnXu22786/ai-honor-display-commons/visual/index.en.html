Overview Map

The package organises an honor-display system and commons component library along the heritage green belt of the former Jingzhang railway. The concept scope is based on the organizer-provided provisional boundary (not a redline); the three-level official scopes are: coordinated research area ~43.6 km², overall design area ~11.4 km², key areas ~368.4 ha. This package is based on the overall design area and deepens the three key areas.Source

Blue-Green & Public Space

Buildings

Renewal Projects
