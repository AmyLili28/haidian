# RAIL-TRACE GALLERY - NORTH RAIL-ART BELT OF THE HERITAGE PARK (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以「轨痕画廊（Rail-Trace Gallery）」组织遗址公园北段铁轨艺术带概念：保留北段铁轨线形作为空间骨架，把百年铁路遗存转译为可漫步、可创作、可参与的艺术文化带，让铁轨的「痕迹」长出现代艺术的「新芽」，构成百年京张AI创新带的北段文化序章；「一轨三台」——轨痕美术馆（铁轨美术馆：遗存展陈+公共艺术工坊）、枕木回声台（枕木剧场：露天演出+回声装置）、道岔星台（道岔广场：分岔记忆+星空舞台），五类AI+场景（铁轨数字孪生导览、AR百年铁路复原体验、艺术装置互动、夜跑光影智能联动、无障碍智慧导览）仅处理匿名聚合数据、关键决策人工复核、禁止过度监控，交通慢行优先、与清河站门户及上地/中关村软件园慢行衔接，四季活动与夜间经济克制引导，分期实施+活动体系（铁轨艺术季、遗址手作市集、钢轨灯光节）。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Rail-Trace Gallery - North Rail-Art Belt of the Heritage Park: keeping the north section's rail alignments as the spatial spine, translating century-old railway heritage into a walkable, creatable, participatory art belt.
- Node 1 - Rail-Trace Gallery: rail gallery (heritage exhibition + public-art workshop).
- Node 2 - Sleeper Echo Stage: sleeper theater (open-air performance + echo installation).
- Node 3 - Switch-Star Plaza: switch-point plaza (fork memory + star stage).
- 5 AI+ scenarios (rail digital-twin guided tour, AR century-railway revival, interactive art installations, night-run smart light choreography, accessible smart wayfinding); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance.
- Slow-traffic-first connections to the Qinghe station gateway and Shangdi / Zhongguancun Software Park; restrained seasonal events and night economy (rail art season, heritage handcraft market, steel-rail light festival).
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
