# JINGZHANG NARRATIVE WAYFINDING BELT - CULTURAL STORYTELLING SIGN SYSTEM (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以「京章叙事导视带（WAYMARK·JZ）」沿京张铁路原线绿带与遗址公园组织一体化文化叙事导视标识系统概念：把京张铁路历史、站点故事与创新带当代叙事转译为可识别、可阅读、可更新的标识体系与慢行体验，「一带三站」——站牌叙事站（老站牌与AR故事标识亭）、道钉符号步道（地面图形标识叙事径）、信号声音亭（铁路信号音与多语语音导览装置），五类AI+场景（多语语音导览与AI翻译、AR历史场景复原、导视设施状态巡检AI、人流密度匿名感知与标识动态调配、史实与内容审核人工复核）仅处理匿名聚合数据、关键决策人工复核、禁止过度监控，交通慢行优先、标识系统与慢行轴一体化，分期实施+年度活动体系（京张叙事导览周、口述史采集工作坊、标识设计共创节）。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Jingzhang Narrative Wayfinding Belt (WAYMARK·JZ): organising an integrated cultural storytelling sign system along the heritage green belt, translating railway history, station stories and the innovation belt's contemporary narrative into a recognisable, readable, renewable signage system and slow-traffic experience.
- Node 1 - Storyboard Stop: signage pavilions themed on old station boards and year markers, supporting image/text, QR and AR story reading.
- Node 2 - Spike Symbol Trail: ground-level narrative trail using spike, sleeper and signal symbols as graphic vocabulary.
- Node 3 - Signal Sound Pavilion: railway signal sounds and multilingual audio guide installations (switchable, volume-controlled).
- 5 AI+ scenarios (multilingual audio guide with AI translation, AR historical reconstruction, inspection and maintenance AI for signage facilities, anonymized density sensing and dynamic sign allocation, human-reviewed historical-fact checking); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance.
- Slow-traffic-first continuous green-belt axis with signage integrated into the slow axis; event traffic organised around rail transit plus shuttle services with phased dispersal; annual event brands: narrative tour week, oral-history collection workshop, signage co-creation festival.
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
