## Outline
- Qinghuayuan Station - Centennial Jingzhang Culture Origin: anchoring a station-city culture quarter on the old Qinghuayuan station heritage, translating the waiting room, platform and Chengfu Road frontage into a walkable, exhibit-ready, participatory quarter.
- Node 1 - Waiting-Room Memory Pod: light-touch conversion of the original waiting room into a memory exhibition hall doubling as an urban reading room and community living room.
- Node 2 - Platform Echo Plaza: culture plaza on the original platform imagery keeping sleeper and rail-gauge paving textures, hosting open-air performances and markets.
- Node 3 - Rail-Shadow Green Gallery: slow-traffic stitching section of Chengfu Road organising a green gallery with rail imagery, linking both sides and the heritage park slow network.
- 5 AI+ scenarios (station digital-archive guided tour, AR Jingzhang history revival, anonymized oral-history collection and curation, community culture event AI scheduling assistance, accessible smart interpretation); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance.
- Slow-traffic-first crossing and signal improvements on Chengfu Road, connecting the heritage park slow network and Wudaokou / Xueyuan Road walking systems; restrained seasonal events and night economy (station memory open day, Jingzhang oral-history market, rail-shadow light season).
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
