# ZHILINK CHAIN VALLEY - ELEMENT CHAIN BELT: Zhongguancun Tech-Service Wing (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以「智枢链谷（ZHILINK CHAIN VALLEY）」组织中关村科技服务翼概念：把科技服务视为组织创新要素流动的「第一链」，沿中关村大街构筑要素全球化配置的科技服务生态带，让技术、资本、人才、数据、标准五类要素沿链流动、在带聚合；三处原创概念点位——知转驿站（科技成果转化服务节点：概念验证+中试加速+成果路演）、金链驿台（投融资与创新资本节点：创投对接+耐心资本聚合+科技金融沙盒）、慧才驿栈（人才与国际合作服务节点：跨境人才一站式服务+全球网络链接），六类AI+场景（科技服务智能匹配与政策合规助手、知识产权价值评估助手、投融资对接助手、人才服务与签证辅导助手、标准与评测数字工具、数据要素流通台账工具）仅处理匿名聚合数据、敏感结论人工复核、禁止个体追踪，分期实施+活动体系（成果转化路演日、技术交易集市、资本对接双周会、国际人才服务日、标准评测开放周、智枢链谷年度论坛）。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Zhilink Chain Valley - Element Chain Belt (Zhongguancun Tech-Service Wing): a tech-service ecosystem belt along Zhongguancun Avenue organizing the global allocation of elements - technology, capital, talent, data and standards flow along the chain and aggregate in the belt.
- Node 1 - Zhizhuan Relay Post: tech-transfer service node (concept validation + pilot acceleration + result roadshow).
- Node 2 - Jinlian Capital Platform: investment & innovation capital node (VC matching, patient capital aggregation, fintech sandbox).
- Node 3 - Huicai Talent Inn: global talent & international cooperation node (one-stop cross-border talent services, global network links).
- 6 AI+ scenarios (service matching & compliance assistant, IP valuation assistant, investment matching assistant, talent & visa assistant, standards & evaluation digital tools, data-element ledger tools); anonymized aggregation only, sensitive conclusions human-reviewed, no individual tracking.
- Five concept mechanisms for global allocation: global innovation network links, technology transfer & IP trading, cross-border talent mobility services, standards & evaluation services, data-element circulation rules.
- Avenue renewal with service nodes integrated into the street public-space sequence; phased implementation and event system (roadshow days, technology trading market, capital matching, global talent service days, standards open week, annual forum).
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
