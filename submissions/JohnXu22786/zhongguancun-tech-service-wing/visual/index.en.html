Buildings

Stock-first, no new large volumes: quality research/business premises and heritage buildings along the avenue are registered and kept as far as possible; functional mixed-use renovation and interface upgrades dominate; only rare dilapidated or seriously obstructing buildings are appraised building by building.

Mobility & Slow Network

Bus-bone plus pedestrian-first sections; last-mile rail transfers networked (Qinghe, Zhichunlu, Wudaokou, Dazhongsi and other existing facilities as anchors); the first Xiaoyuehe experience-path segment is in the near-term plan.

Blue-Green & Public Space

Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park green spine + Xiaoyuehe blue-green branch + Xueyuan Road university green belt form the network; relay plazas act as breathing points; a reversible public-space component library (street furniture, seating, information columns) rotates by season; smart street furniture and lighting follow a restraint principle.

Renewal Projects

Three-phased concept list: near 1-3y digital base + AI copilot 1.0, pilot micro-renewal, relay siting and events; mid 3-5y relays online, avenue sequence linked, mixed-use pilots; far 5-10y full-chain ecosystem, offshore liaison nodes and global networks routine. Each phase has a minimum viable pilot, resource pack, milestones and go/no-go and stop conditions with a four-actor RACI; no implementation commitment.

AI Scenarios

Six AI+ families: smart matching & compliance (licensed-professional review), IP valuation (human review), investment matching (human review of credit and due diligence), talent & visa (human fallback), standards & evaluation tools, and a data ledger; the scenario-card index holds 10 cards. Anonymized aggregates only, local de-identification of personal and business-sensitive information, no individual tracking, no over-monitoring; AI-governance three-liner: anonymized aggregation / human review of key decisions / no over-monitoring. Three AI technical test protocols are documented in the report.
