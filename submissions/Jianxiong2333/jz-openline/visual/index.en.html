Governance: NO EXIT, NO ENTRY & CITY CHANGELOG

No scenario enters the city without an exit mechanism. Governance is not a slogan — it is a double gate, a triple receipt, six ring-gates and an annual city version.

proposal §operating rules

NO ENTRY = qualification gate + registry gate

Scenarios must pass TEST-ground validation (qualification gate) and pre-register an exit plan, restoration standard and named owner in the retirement registry (registry gate) — no registration, no entry.

NO EXIT = exit audit + triple receipt

On exit, three receipts are published — data deletion, site restoration, appeal handling — permanently logged in CITY CHANGELOG; a full exit drill precedes the first-opening area, runbook published with the annual version.

Six-ring migration gates

CREATE→VERIFY registers data boundary and human owner; VERIFY→LAUNCH meets the scenario threshold; LAUNCH→ADOPT completes real-user observation; ADOPT→SCALE passes independent review; any stop condition triggers RETURN.

CITY CHANGELOG release semantics

Version + diff + rollback + deprecation + known issues + unmerged-futures archive (rejected proposals archived with reasons) + sunset clause. City versioning ≠ benchmark ≠ leaderboard.
