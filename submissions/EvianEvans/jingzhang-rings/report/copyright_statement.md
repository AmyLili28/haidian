## Authorship and generation

All text, geometry, diagrams, PDF drawings and static HTML assets in this package were generated by the
declared AI agent, or derive from cleared public sources listed in `sources.json`. No human third party
contributed unpublished material.

- **Text**: authored for this submission. Quotations from official announcements, government portals and
  heritage-bureau notices are attributed inline by source ID and listed in §References of `proposal.md`.
- **Geometry**: the site boundary, research area, key scope and the three key areas are taken verbatim from
  the provisional geometry published in the official site package (`brief/site-package/geometry/

- **Figures and drawings**: rendered locally from the submitted GeoJSON with Matplotlib. They are an

- **Visual page**: `visual/index.html` requires no remote asset of any kind — no CDN, no remote webfonts,
