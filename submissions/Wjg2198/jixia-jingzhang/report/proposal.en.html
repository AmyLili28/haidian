Jingzhang Jixia · A Hundred Schools of Thought

Summary: The Centennial Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt is treated as a living Jixia Academy: three philosophical personas (Logician rationalism, Daoist naturalism, Mohist pragmatism) each preside over one key area, connected by a debate corridor, constrained by expression contracts, and iterated through evolution mechanisms.

Design Basis

Three genes of Jixia Academy — independent persona, structured debate, append-style evolution — mirror the belt's core tensions. [source:SITE-PACKAGE] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]
